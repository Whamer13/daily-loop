YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy notice.

This is the starter privacy notice for Daily Loop. The site owner must review and update it so it accurately describes the live website, hosting provider, advertising setup and contact details before launch.

Information stored on your device

Daily Loop's favourites feature stores the names of your saved games in your browser's local storage. This is used to remember your choices on that device. The site does not currently require an account to browse the directory.

Advertising and cookies

The supplied starter files contain inactive advertising placeholders. No advertising tags are enabled by default. If the site owner later adds an advertising provider such as Google AdSense, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information about visits and devices to provide, measure or personalise ads. The owner must update this notice to identify the providers and explain the actual choices available to visitors.

For visitors in the UK, EEA or Switzerland, the owner must configure an appropriate consent solution before serving personalised advertising. Google states that publishers serving personalised ads in the UK and EEA must use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. See Google's publisher consent requirements.

Third-party game websites

When you click a Play link, you leave Daily Loop and visit an independently operated website. That website has its own privacy notice, cookies and data practices. Daily Loop does not control them; please review the destination site's information.

Hosting and server logs

The website host may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and access times in server logs for security and operation. The owner should check the chosen host's terms and privacy documentation and update this section to match the actual arrangement.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@yourdomain.com (replace this placeholder before publishing).
