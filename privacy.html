YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy notice.

This policy explains how Daily Loop handles information when you visit the website, including information processed by third-party services used to operate and display the site.

Information stored on your device

Daily Loop's favourites feature stores the names of your saved games in your browser's local storage. This is used to remember your choices on that device. The site does not currently require an account to browse the directory.

Fonts

The Daily Loop homepage uses Google Fonts to display text in DM Sans and DM Mono. When you visit the homepage, your browser requests font files from Google's servers. Google necessarily receives your IP address to deliver these files. Google states that information collected through Google Fonts is not used to create profiles of visitors or for advertising. Read Google's explanation of Google Fonts privacy.

Advertising and cookies

Daily Loop does not currently display advertisements. Although advertising placeholders may appear on the website, no advertising provider or advertising scripts are currently enabled. Daily Loop therefore does not currently use advertising cookies or similar technologies. If advertising is introduced in future, this policy will be updated to identify the providers, explain how information is used and describe the choices available to visitors.

Before introducing personalised advertising, Daily Loop will need to consider the applicable consent requirements. Google requires publishers serving personalised ads to users in the UK, EEA or Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. See Google's publisher consent requirements.

Third-party game websites

When you click a Play link, you leave Daily Loop and visit an independently operated website. That website has its own privacy notice, cookies and data practices. Daily Loop does not control them; please review the destination site's information.

Hosting and server logs

Daily Loop is hosted by Netlify. When you visit the website, Netlify may process technical information about your requests to deliver, operate and secure the site. This may include your IP address, the pages or files requested, the time of the request and information about your browser or device. For more information, read Netlify's Privacy Statement and Netlify's documentation on website request monitoring.
