GOOD TO KNOW

Daily games, explained.

What counts as a daily game?

A daily game usually offers a new shared puzzle or challenge on a regular daily schedule. Some also include practice modes, archives or extra rounds; the daily challenge is the main reason they are listed here.

Do I play the games on Daily Loop?

No. Daily Loop is a directory. Play links take you to the independent website that runs each game. That site controls its own rules, accounts, availability, advertising and privacy practices.

Are all the games free?

Many offer a free daily challenge, but access rules can change and some sites may require an account or offer paid extras. Check the destination website before signing up or paying for anything.

Does this directory include every daily game?

No. New games launch frequently and some disappear or change address. This is a curated collection that will need regular updates, not a complete list of every daily game on the internet.

How are favourites saved?

Your favourite game names are saved in local storage in the browser you are using. They are not synced between devices and can be cleared by removing this site's browser data.
